Leadership Principles

Leadership That Puts Children First

Listen

Understanding the unique needs of pupils, parents and teachers through attentive communication.

Guide

Providing clear educational direction and support for the entire school community.

Encourage

Creating an environment where children and teachers feel confident to try their best.

Lead by Example

Demonstrating the respect, discipline, and integrity expected of our pupils every day.

School Support

Leadership Supports the Whole School

Strong school leadership is not only about administration. It is about creating the conditions where teachers can teach well and children can learn, participate and grow.
