Why Dan-Gracious Land?

We believe children thrive when they have the right environment to learn, explore, participate and build confidence.

Strong Academic Foundation

Helping children develop essential literacy, numeracy, and problem-solving skills needed to progress with confidence.

Caring Learning Environment

Activities & Experiences

Creativity & Discovery

Individual Growth

Simple & Transparent

How Admission Works

Four straightforward steps to enrol your child at Dan-Gracious Land.

01

Make an Enquiry

Contact the school using our quick online enquiry form, direct telephone line, or WhatsApp.

02

Speak With the School

Our admissions team provides relevant information, schedules a physical visit, and answers your questions.

03

Complete Registration

Fill out the required registration forms and submit essential records (e.g. birth certificate, photos).

04

Welcome to DGL

Once admission is confirmed, your child receives their uniform list, class assignment, and official start date.

Checklist

What You'll Need

Standard documents required to complete registration.

Required Documents

• Completed admission registration form

• Copy of child's official birth certificate or age declaration

• Recent passport photographs of the pupil

Previous School Records

• Recent report cards or academic progress reports from previous school (where applicable)

• Transfer letter (for pupils transferring into primary classes)

Parent/Guardian Info

• Passport photograph of parent/guardian

• Valid contact phone numbers & WhatsApp details

• Emergency contact address and information
