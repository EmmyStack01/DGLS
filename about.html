Our Heritage

Our Story

It started with a simple belief: that education in early childhood should do more than prepare children for exams—it should give them confidence, build their character, and nurture their natural curiosity.

Dan-Gracious Land Nursery & Primary School was established in Idimu, Lagos, to meet a distinct need within our local community—a school where parents could be confident that their children are truly known, cared for, and challenged to achieve their personal best.

Over the years, our school has grown into a warm, active learning environment. While our facilities and activities have developed, our core commitment remains unchanged: providing a supportive foundation where every pupil is encouraged to learn confidently, participate fully, and develop values that serve them for a lifetime.

Our Core Values

What We Believe

Education is about more than what a child can remember for an examination. It is also about confidence, character, curiosity and the ability to discover what they can do.

Excellence

Encouraging children to give their best in their academic work, creative projects, and personal conduct.

Character

Helping children develop personal discipline, honesty, mutual respect, and responsibility in daily life.

Confidence

Creating an environment where children feel comfortable expressing themselves, asking questions, and participating.

Curiosity

Encouraging pupils to explore new concepts, solve problems, and develop a natural passion for learning.

Responsibility

Teaching children to understand their role in their classroom, support their peers, and care for their community.

Growth

Recognising that every child develops at their own pace and providing steady, patient guidance along the way.

Educational Philosophy

Learning Beyond the Classroom

"We want children to experience learning, not simply memorise it."

Strong Foundations

Building essential literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking skills required for academic progression.

Participation

Giving pupils opportunities to take part in sports, cultural events, field trips, and school competitions.

Creativity

Allowing children to explore artistic ideas, perform, express themselves, and discover individual talents.

Collaboration

Helping children learn how to work effectively with classmates, respect diverse views, and contribute.

Moral Character

Supporting the development of good habits, self-discipline, respect for elders, and kindness toward peers in everyday interactions.
